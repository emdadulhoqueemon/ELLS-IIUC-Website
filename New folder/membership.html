COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Are students from non-humanities majors eligible to join?

Absolutely. Over 40% of our active fellows study Computer Science, Biology, Economics, and Engineering. The only prerequisite is an authentic passion for writing, reading, and linguistic inquiry.

Are fee waivers available for student dues?

Yes. If dues present a financial hardship, select "Student Fellow" and indicate in your statement of intent. The Society maintains an endowed fellowship fund to ensure cost is never a barrier.

How often are poetry and writing workshops convened?

The Keats Poetry Fellowship meets every Tuesday evening, and fiction/memoir workshops meet every Thursday. You are free to attend as many circles as your schedule allows.
