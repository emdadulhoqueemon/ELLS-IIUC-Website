Four Specialized Literary Wings

ELLS nurtures creative writing, critical theory, linguistics, and public speaking across four dedicated student-led guilds.

Creative Expression

Poetry & Slam Guild

Hosts spoken-word recitals, sonnet workshops, and dramatic monologues inspired by classical and modern verse.

Language & Research

Linguistics Forum

Explores phonetics, sociolinguistics, ELT pedagogy, and academic paper presentations for FAHIIUCIC.

Editorial & Press

The Scribe Review

The official departmental wall-magazine and journal publishing student essays, book reviews, and criticism.

Reading Seminars

The Great Books Circle

Bi-weekly roundtable discussions dissecting world classics, postcolonial fiction, and literary theory.
