OUR HERITAGE & ETHOS

Cultivating Eloquence, Curiosity, & Critical Fellowship

The English Language and Literary Society (ELLS) is a premier platform dedicated to nurturing the creative and intellectual potential of students. We foster a vibrant community where linguistic curiosity meets literary expression, empowering scholars to explore, debate, and elevate their communication skills through collaborative events and academic excellence.

FOUNDING PRINCIPLES

Our Mission & Guiding Creed

We believe literature and language are living instruments that challenge complacency, cultivate human empathy, and illuminate complex truths.

Intellectual Freedom

Fostering fearless, respectful discourse where students interrogate texts, challenge established canons, and explore experimental poetics in an open environment.

The Craft of Precision

From syntactical nuance and rhetorical persuasion to lyrical prosody, we view linguistic mastery as essential to clear reasoning and civic engagement.

Generous Fellowship

Literature flourishes in community. We champion mentorship, celebrate nascent voices, and support fellow writers through every phase of the creative struggle.

CHRONOLOGY

Nearly a Century of Literary History

From our modest beginnings in a candlelit library alcove to our dynamic contemporary fellowship.

1928

The First Salon in Divinity Hall

Twelve undergraduate scholars and two literature professors gathered around a fireplace to discuss modern poetry and Shakespearean soliloquies, officially forming the English Language and Literary Society.

1954

Founding of The Scribe Review

The Society established its print gazette, publishing early works by students who later became distinguished playwrights, journalists, and Booker Prize nominees.

1982

Inauguration of Linguistics & Semiotics Forum

Recognizing the expanding frontiers of cognitive linguistics and structural rhetoric, the society expanded its scope beyond classical literature to linguistic science.

2005

The Annual Inter-Collegiate Poetry Slam

ELLS hosted the region's first inter-university spoken-word tournament, drawing hundreds of competing poets and inaugurating an annual campus tradition.

2026

A Thriving Multi-Disciplinary Guild

Today, with over 1,450 active fellows, student publications, workshops, and digital archives, ELLS Club remains a vibrant nexus of creative and academic life.

SPECIALIZED CIRCLES

Find Your Circle Within ELLS

Every member has a distinct voice. Join one or more of our dedicated weekly focus circles.

The Keats Poetry Circle

Weekly salon devoted to verse analysis, meter drills, and candid peer workshop sessions. Every meeting concludes with open readings and feedback.

Meets Tuesdays 6:30 PM

Rhetoric & Discourse Guild

Engages with the mechanics of argumentative prose, forensics, persuasive speechwriting, and the rhetorical strategies of great historical orators.

Meets Thursdays 5:00 PM

The Scribe Editorial Collective

Offers hands-on training in substantive editing, proofreading, typography, and layout production for our published anthologies and journals.

Bi-Weekly Saturdays

The Comparative Classics Round

Deep-dive reading sessions into major literary masterpieces, world fiction in translation, and philosophical fiction spanning ancient to modern eras.

Meets Alternate Fridays
OUR SOCIAL COMPACT

Code of Intellectual Discourse

At ELLS Club, we hold discourse to be a sacred art. All members subscribe to three essential rules of engagement:

  • Generous Listening: Every participant listens attentively to fellow perspectives before formulating critique or response.
  • Constructive Criticism: Feedback on written work targets the craft, structure, and artistic voice—never the character of the author.
  • Diversity of Thought & Background: We celebrate varied dialects, cultural storytelling forms, and linguistic traditions as rich assets to our shared craft.
